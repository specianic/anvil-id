# Phase 07.1 Plan 05: UX journey helpers Summary

The review's exploratory Playwright helpers now live in tests/e2e/lib as typed, assertion-based modules (cached journey clients, PKCE authorize builder, a fake RP on a configurable port, console sign-in/nav, overflow, focus and no-dead-end assertions), proven by a green smoke spec.

## What was built

- **lib/ux-state.ts**: `uxState(browser)` reuses `sharedManagementCredential` and caches three default-tenant clients (registered against the fake RP origin; the cache is rebuilt if the origin changes). `authorizeUrl(client, extra, issuer)` returns `{ url, verifier, state, nonce }` (S256). `fakeRp()` serves a small page with `#rp-path` and `#rp-params` (HTML-escaped). `newUser`, `atRp`, `token`, `visit` as in the review scripts.
- **lib/console-ux.ts**: `consoleSignIn(page, admin)` (reuses `consoleUrl` and `login`), `nav(page, label)` for the grouped and mobile nav from 07.1-03, `mainText(page)`.
- **lib/layout.ts**: `expectNoHorizontalOverflow(page, width = 390)` lists the elements that stick out when it fails. `expectFocusVisible(page)` checks for an outline or box-shadow on the focused element. `expectWayForward(page)` looks for `#link-continue`, `#link-account`, `#link-login`, `#form-sign-out` submit or `main a[href]`.
- **specs/ux-helpers.spec.ts**: (1) a cached web client and a new user reach the fake RP with `code` and the expected `state`; (2) `/ui/login` at 390px has no overflow and no axe violations.

Dropped: screenshots, `dump`, `capture`, `note` and the review state files. No local paths are committed (checked with grep).
