  <behavior>
    - After each of these requests the app's wake Notify holds a permit (`tokio::time::timeout(100ms, wake.notified())` is Ok): RP-initiated /logout with a session, /ui/logout confirm, a password sign-in, an upstream or email sign-in (one is enough; they share signed_in_response), account-page "sign out" of another session, "sign out all others", password change with sign-out of others, recovery completion, admin DELETE session, admin revoke-all, admin DELETE user
    - A GET of the account sessions page leaves no permit (the helper is not called on read paths)
    - End to end: worker with a 1 h poll on the app's Notify; alice signs in to a back-channel client, then bob signs in in the same browser (session switch) -> the RP receives alice's logout token within 5 s
  </behavior>
  <action>
In `state.rs`, add `pub fn wake_logout_worker(&self)`. Doc: "revocations enqueue back-channel deliveries in their own storage
transaction; this wakes the worker so they go out now instead of at the next poll. Spurious wakes cost one claim query."
Replace the call in `ui/logout.rs` with it. Keep `logout_wake()` for the binary and tests.

Call the helper after a successful revoking call in:
- `account_sessions.rs` (both handlers);
- `account_profile.rs` (when the password change revoked others, or always after a successful change);
- `recovery.rs` (after the recovery submit; the flow outcome is opaque there, so after every POST of that route is acceptable,
  and a comment must say why);
- `admin/sessions.rs` (both);
- `admin/users.rs` (delete).

In `login.rs`, call it at the start of `signed_in_response`: every sign-in can switch away from another user's session, and
waking on each sign-in is cheaper than threading a "revoked" flag out of the flow engine.

Never wake from read-only handlers.

`tests/support/mod.rs`: build with `AppState::new` + `anvil_web::router(state.clone())`, keep the `Arc<Notify>` from
`state.logout_wake()` and expose `pub fn logout_wake(&self) -> Arc<Notify>`. No other behaviour change: every existing test
binary must still compile and pass, and this is the only plan in the wave that edits support/mod.rs.

Write `tests/backchannel_wake.rs` with the behaviour cases. Reuse the patterns of `backchannel.rs` (fixture, wiremock RP,
worker construction with `poll = 1 h`) by copying the small helpers; do not edit backchannel.rs (04-34 owns it).

e2e: reduce the 30 s back-channel waits for the switch case in `prompt.spec.ts` / `nojs-logout.spec.ts` to the normal logout
budget, and update their comments, which name the 10 s poll. Type-check with tsc.
