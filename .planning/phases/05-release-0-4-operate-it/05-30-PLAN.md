<objective>
Gap closure for the 05-11 deferred item: under MFA `required`, a user whose only second factor is a passkey (for example one
enrolled in-flow) and who signs in with a password is sent to enrolment again, because `user_has_factor` counts TOTP only and
the second-factor step accepts no passkey assertion. Make the second-factor step accept a passkey bound to the step's user,
count passkeys as a second factor everywhere, and prove every refusal with a negative test.

Purpose: 05-11 lets users enrol a passkey under `required`; without this they cannot use it after a password sign-in.
Output: core flow changes, two routes, second-factor page section with en/de keys, tests, e2e step, docs.
</objective>
